About measurements

Changes are relative to calibration. ° = degrees; unitless values are ratios of body proportions. Camera proximity uses relative face size, not physical screen distance. Shoulder raise, elbow spread, forward head, and proximity flag increases only. Missing measurements are excluded from scores.

Hand tracking

Put a small blue marker near the pen tip and a green marker farther up the shaft, with both visible. Use the same pen and marker positions during calibration and writing. These markers measure the visible shaft, not contact with the page.

Compares finger, marked-pen, and wrist measurements with a personal reference. Pressure requires a calibrated sensor; camera proximity does not establish contact.
